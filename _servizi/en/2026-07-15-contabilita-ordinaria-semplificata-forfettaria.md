<p>Your accounting regime decides how many records you keep, how you calculate income and how much time you spend on paperwork. For a freelancer the choice is usually between three options.</p><h2>Flat-rate: the lightest</h2><p>No VAT registers and no full bookkeeping: you keep your invoices and calculate tax by applying a coefficient to revenue. It can only be used below a revenue threshold (85,000 euro a year) and with some requirements to check.</p><h2>Simplified: essential records</h2><p>Income is calculated as the difference between revenue and actual costs. You keep VAT registers and the documents for your costs. It is worth it when expenses are high and the flat-rate coefficient would penalise you.</p><h2>Ordinary: for those who grow</h2><p>It is full accounting, with a journal and entries. It is mandatory for limited companies and beyond certain sizes, while a freelancer rarely gets there.</p><h2>How to choose</h2><ul><li>Few costs? Usually the flat-rate regime pays less tax.</li><li>Lots of expenses and investments? Compare it with the simplified regime.</li><li>Expecting to exceed the thresholds? Plan the switch in time.</li></ul><h2>How Fisco Perfetto helps you</h2><p>We explain your situation in plain words and tell you what to do, in what order and by which deadlines. Tax rules change often: before deciding, always check your case with a consultant.</p>
