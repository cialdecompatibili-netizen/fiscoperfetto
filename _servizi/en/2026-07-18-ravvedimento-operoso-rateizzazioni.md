<p>It happens: a missed deadline, a forgotten F24. Ravvedimento operoso lets you regularise voluntarily by paying much lower penalties than the ordinary ones.</p><h2>How it works</h2><p>You pay the amount due plus statutory interest and a reduced penalty, calculated on the days of delay. The sooner you act, the less you pay.</p><h2>When it is worth it</h2><p>Almost always, if the Revenue Agency has not already sent you a notice. Waiting makes the penalty rise at every step.</p><h2>What if you do not have the money</h2><p>For significant sums there are instalment options. Better to ask for them in advance than to face forced collection.</p><div class="art-nota"><p>As soon as you notice the delay, calculate the ravvedimento. Every extra day changes the amount.</p></div>
