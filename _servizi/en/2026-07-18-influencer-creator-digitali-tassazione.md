<p>Occasional earnings are not a business activity, but when collaborations become continuous the VAT number is practically unavoidable. Better to understand it before the tax authorities do.</p><h2>When you need a VAT number</h2><p>When the fees are habitual, organised and repeated: sponsorships, affiliations, product sales, platform payments. The occasional nature applies only to truly isolated episodes.</p><h2>Which code and which regime</h2><p>It depends on the type of activity: content, advertising, sales. Often the flat-rate regime is the most convenient for those starting out.</p><h2>Payments from abroad</h2><p>Platforms and foreign brands often pay without VAT: you need to issue an invoice with the correct wording and keep track of every payment received.</p><div class="art-nota"><p>Keep contracts and engagement letters: they are the proof of what you invoiced and why.</p></div>
