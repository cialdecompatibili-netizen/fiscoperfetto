<p>Craftspeople and shopkeepers have a dedicated social security scheme, with fixed contributions and a variable share on income. It is not the Separate Management, and the cost is different.</p><h2>Who must register</h2><p>Owners of craft businesses and commercial activities. Registration goes through the Business Register by means of ComUnica, at the same time as opening.</p><h2>Fixed and variable contributions</h2><p>A minimum is paid even with low earnings, plus a percentage of income above a threshold. Under the flat-rate regime there is also a contribution reduction, if you request it.</p><h2>Deadlines</h2><p>Four fixed instalments during the year, plus the balance and advance on income with the tax return. Mark the dates on your calendar.</p><div class="art-nota"><p>The minimum contribution is a certain cost: include it in your monthly expense plan right away, even in the months when you invoice little.</p></div>
