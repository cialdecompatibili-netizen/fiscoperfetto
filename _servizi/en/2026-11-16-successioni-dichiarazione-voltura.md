<p>After the loss of a loved one there are tax and administrative tasks to handle too. We help you put them in order, without rushing and in clear language.</p><h2>What it includes</h2><ul><li>Succession return and its attachments.</li><li>Land registry transfer of properties.</li><li>Check of bank accounts, securities and other assets to declare.</li><li>Guidance on any taxes due.</li></ul><h2>Useful documents</h2><p>Death certificate, heirs' details, any will, property searches and deeds, statements of bank accounts and securities.</p><h2>Timing</h2><p>The return has filing deadlines set by law. It is best to contact us early, so we can check together the deadlines that apply to your case.</p><h2>How Fisco Perfetto helps you</h2><p>We explain your situation in plain words and tell you what to do, in what order and by which deadlines. Tax rules change often: before deciding, always check your case with a consultant.</p>
