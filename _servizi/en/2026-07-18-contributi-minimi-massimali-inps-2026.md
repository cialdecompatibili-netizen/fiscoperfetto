<p>Contributions are the item that surprises most people opening a VAT number. The mechanism depends on the scheme you are registered with, and the amounts are updated every year.</p><h2>Craftspeople and shopkeepers</h2><p>You pay a fixed share, the minimum, even if you earn little in a year. If income exceeds the minimum threshold you pay an additional percentage on the excess, up to the maximum. A reduction may be available for those under the flat-rate regime, to be requested from INPS.</p><h2>Separate Management</h2><p>For professionals without a dedicated fund, contributions are a percentage of income: if you do not earn, you do not pay. There is no fixed minimum.</p><h2>What minimum and maximum mean</h2><ul><li>The minimum is the base on which you pay in any case.</li><li>The maximum is the ceiling: above that income you no longer pay contributions.</li></ul><h2>How much to set aside</h2><p>Put aside a share of every payment you receive for taxes and contributions. You can find the exact amounts for your year on the INPS website: check them before doing your sums.</p><h2>How Fisco Perfetto helps you</h2><p>We explain your situation in plain words and tell you what to do, in what order and by which deadlines. Tax rules change often: before deciding, always check your case with a consultant.</p>
