<p>The ATECO code looks like a bureaucratic detail, but it carries many consequences: the profitability coefficient in the flat-rate regime, the pension fund, sometimes the obligation to join a professional register. Choosing it at random is the most common mistake.</p><h2>Start from what you really do</h2><p>Describe in one sentence what you sell and to whom. Then look in the official table for the code that matches your main activity, not the one you hope to do in two years.</p><h2>Check coefficient and fund</h2><p>Two similar codes can have very different coefficients, and therefore different taxes for the same turnover. Also check whether the activity falls under a professional fund or the INPS Separate Management.</p><h2>You can have more than one</h2><p>If you carry out different activities you can list several: one will be the main one. The coefficient applies to the revenue of each code, so keep your invoices clearly separate.</p><div class="art-nota"><p>You can change the code later, but it requires a communication and sometimes changes the tax due. Better one extra check at the start.</p></div>
