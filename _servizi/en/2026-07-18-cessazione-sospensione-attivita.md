<p>You can close a VAT number at any time, but taxes and contributions for the final period remain due. Closing badly means finding yourself with notices and penalties months later.</p><h2>What to communicate</h2><p>The closure must be communicated to the Revenue Agency and, if you registered, to the Business Register and INPS. The deadlines are short, so respect them.</p><h2>The final obligations</h2><p>Issue your last invoices, pay the year's taxes and contributions, and file the tax return for the period of activity.</p><h2>If you only want to stop</h2><p>If you are thinking of starting again, consider whether to keep it open at minimal cost: under some regimes there are no fixed expenses, under others there are. It depends on your INPS position.</p><div class="art-nota"><p>Before closing, check contributions and taxes still to be paid: it is better to settle them right away than to face a tax notice two years later.</p></div>
