<p>The CU is the document by which whoever pays you certifies the fees paid and the tax withheld during the year. If you work with clients who apply withholding tax, you receive it every year.</p><h2>Who issues it</h2><p>The withholding agent, i.e. the client who paid you fees and withheld the tax. If you are flat-rate and invoice without withholding, you normally do not receive one.</p><h2>What to check</h2><ul><li>Your tax code and personal data.</li><li>The amount of the fees, which must match your invoices.</li><li>The withholdings, which you must be able to deduct from the taxes due.</li></ul><h2>What it is for</h2><p>The CU data flows into your tax return and the pre-filled return. If something does not add up, ask the client for a corrected version.</p><h2>When it arrives</h2><p>In the first months of the following year. Submission deadlines can change from year to year, so check the ones in force.</p><h2>How Fisco Perfetto helps you</h2><p>We explain your situation in plain words and tell you what to do, in what order and by which deadlines. Tax rules change often: before deciding, always check your case with a consultant.</p>
