<p>VAT is the most uncomfortable part of life with a VAT number outside the flat-rate regime. Understanding the cycle spares you omissions and penalties.</p><h2>Monthly or quarterly</h2><p>Monthly settlement is the rule. Below certain revenue thresholds you can switch to quarterly, which shifts payments but adds a small surcharge on the amount due.</p><h2>The year-end advance</h2><p>In December you pay an advance on the VAT of the last period, calculated with one of the available methods. It stops you being unprepared for the balance.</p><h2>The annual return</h2><p>It summarises the year's VAT: sales and purchases, payments and the final credit or debit. It is filed once a year, electronically.</p><h2>If you are flat-rate</h2><p>You do not charge VAT on invoices and do not make settlements. When you leave the regime, all of this becomes part of your routine.</p><h2>How Fisco Perfetto helps you</h2><p>We explain your situation in plain words and tell you what to do, in what order and by which deadlines. Tax rules change often: before deciding, always check your case with a consultant.</p>
