<p>The Separate Management is the social security position for those who work on their own without being registered with a professional fund. Contributions are calculated on income, not on turnover.</p><h2>Who must register</h2><p>Consultants, freelancers, collaborators and other professionals whose activity has no dedicated fund. Registration happens automatically with the VAT number.</p><h2>How it is calculated</h2><p>The rate applies to taxable income up to the annual ceiling. Under the flat-rate regime income comes from the coefficient, so contributions also depend on your ATECO code.</p><h2>When you pay</h2><p>Balance and advances follow the deadlines for income taxes, using the F24 form. It is worth setting aside a share every month so there are no surprises.</p><div class="art-nota"><p>Contributions are not just a cost: they build your pension. Check your contribution statement at least once a year.</p></div>
