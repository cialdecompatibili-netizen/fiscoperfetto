<p>Almost every tax obligation today goes through a digital login. Setting them up at the start spares you blockages at the wrong moment.</p><h2>PEC</h2><p>It is certified email with legal value. For many activities it is compulsory and it is used to communicate with public bodies and clients.</p><h2>SPID or CIE</h2><p>They are the digital identities you use to access the services of the Revenue Agency, INPS and the public administration.</p><h2>Digital signature</h2><p>It lets you sign documents with legal value, for example contracts and online filings.</p><h2>Delegations</h2><p>If you work with a consultant, you can authorise them to access your tax services, such as the tax drawer and e-invoices. The delegation can be revoked at any time.</p><h2>How Fisco Perfetto helps you</h2><p>We explain your situation in plain words and tell you what to do, in what order and by which deadlines. Tax rules change often: before deciding, always check your case with a consultant.</p>
