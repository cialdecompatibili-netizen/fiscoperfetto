<p>Two situations often confuse those who are starting out: issuing the invoice after delivery and buying from suppliers in other European countries.</p><h2>The deferred invoice</h2><p>If you deliver goods or services and document the transaction with a transport document, you can issue a summary invoice later, by the 15th day of the following month.</p><h2>Purchases from EU suppliers</h2><p>As a rule you handle the VAT yourself using the reverse charge mechanism: you supplement the invoice received with the Italian rate and record it.</p><h2>Deadlines and submission</h2><p>The integrated invoice must be issued within the month following receipt and transmitted through the Exchange System.</p><h2>Common mistakes</h2><ul><li>Forgetting to supplement the invoice.</li><li>Recording it in the wrong period.</li><li>Not keeping the supplier's original document.</li></ul><h2>How Fisco Perfetto helps you</h2><p>We explain your situation in plain words and tell you what to do, in what order and by which deadlines. Tax rules change often: before deciding, always check your case with a consultant.</p>
