<p>Paying less tax legally means knowing what you can deduct. It depends on the regime you are in, so the first question is always that one.</p><h2>If you are under the flat-rate regime</h2><p>Costs are not deducted one by one: income is calculated with a coefficient on revenue. The compulsory social security contributions you pay remain deductible.</p><h2>If you are simplified or ordinary</h2><p>You deduct costs related to the activity, such as equipment, software, training and professional expenses, if documented and accrued in the year.</p><h2>Personal deductions</h2><p>Some private expenses give the right to a tax deduction on your return, for example medical ones or mortgage interest. You need receipts and traceable payments.</p><h2>A practical tip</h2><p>Keep every invoice and receipt in a single digital folder, split by year. When the return arrives, you will not have to chase anything.</p><h2>How Fisco Perfetto helps you</h2><p>We explain your situation in plain words and tell you what to do, in what order and by which deadlines. Tax rules change often: before deciding, always check your case with a consultant.</p>
