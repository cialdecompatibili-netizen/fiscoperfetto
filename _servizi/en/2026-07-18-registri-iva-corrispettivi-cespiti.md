<p>Outside the flat-rate regime you must record transactions for VAT purposes. It is not difficult if you do it regularly.</p><h2>The main registers</h2><ul><li>Register of issued invoices.</li><li>Purchase register.</li><li>Register of daily takings, if you sell to private individuals without an invoice.</li></ul><h2>Assets and depreciation</h2><p>Assets that last over time, such as computers or equipment, are not fully deducted at once: the cost is spread over several years using the depreciation coefficients provided.</p><h2>Record on time</h2><p>Invoices must be entered within the legal deadlines. Recording as you go avoids mistakes in the VAT settlement.</p><h2>If you are flat-rate</h2><p>You are exempt from VAT registers, but you must still keep invoices and documents.</p><h2>How Fisco Perfetto helps you</h2><p>We explain your situation in plain words and tell you what to do, in what order and by which deadlines. Tax rules change often: before deciding, always check your case with a consultant.</p>
