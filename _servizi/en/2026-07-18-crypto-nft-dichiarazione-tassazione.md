<p>If you have sold, exchanged or received crypto-assets, the tax authorities want to know. The first step is to keep track of every transaction.</p><h2>What may be taxable</h2><ul><li>The sale or conversion that produces a capital gain.</li><li>Some payments received in crypto for an activity.</li><li>Income from staking or similar activities, to be assessed case by case.</li></ul><h2>Tax monitoring</h2><p>Crypto-assets you hold must be reported in the dedicated section of the return, even if held on foreign platforms. Omission carries specific penalties.</p><h2>What to keep</h2><p>Dates, amounts, value in euro at the time of the transaction and the platform used. Without a complete history it is very hard to calculate the gain.</p><h2>Watch the rates</h2><p>Rules and rates on crypto-assets have changed in recent years: check those in force for the year you declare.</p><h2>How Fisco Perfetto helps you</h2><p>We explain your situation in plain words and tell you what to do, in what order and by which deadlines. Tax rules change often: before deciding, always check your case with a consultant.</p>
