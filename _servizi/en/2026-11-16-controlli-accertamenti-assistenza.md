<p>Getting a letter from the Revenue Agency or the collection agent is stressful. Almost always the first step is to read it carefully and check the deadlines to reply.</p><h2>What we do for you</h2><ul><li>We analyse the notice you received and understand what it is about.</li><li>We check whether the claim is correct, using your documents.</li><li>We prepare the reply or the documents to submit.</li><li>We assess the options: payment, instalments, correction, appeal.</li></ul><h2>Most common notices</h2><p>Courtesy notices and irregularity letters, invitations to a hearing, assessment notices, payment demands.</p><h2>Mind the deadlines</h2><p>Each notice has precise and different deadlines to reply or challenge it. Do not let the days pass: bring us the notice as soon as you receive it.</p><h2>How Fisco Perfetto helps you</h2><p>We explain your situation in plain words and tell you what to do, in what order and by which deadlines. Tax rules change often: before deciding, always check your case with a consultant.</p>
