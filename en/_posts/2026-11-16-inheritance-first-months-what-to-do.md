<p>After losing a loved one there are tax matters to handle too. The good news is that there is usually time: the <strong>succession return</strong> (dichiarazione di successione) must be filed within <strong>12 months</strong> from the opening of the succession, which normally coincides with the day of death. This guide puts in order what to do, without rushing but without missing deadlines.</p><h2 id="who">Who must file</h2><p>The heirs and legatees, or their representatives. It is filed electronically with the Revenue Agency, usually together with the land registry transfer request if there are properties. For successions opened from 1 January 2025 some calculation and payment rules changed (Legislative Decree 139/2024): check the ones in force for your case.</p><h2 id="first-steps">First steps</h2><ol><li><strong>Gather documents</strong>: death certificate, heirs' tax codes, any will, property searches and deeds, statements of bank accounts and securities at the date of death, list of debts and expenses.</li><li><strong>Identify the heirs</strong> and their shares, by law or by will.</li><li><strong>List the assets</strong>: houses, land, accounts, securities, cars, policies, shareholdings.</li><li><strong>File the return</strong> within 12 months and request the property transfers.</li><li><strong>Pay the taxes</strong> at the deadlines set by the procedure.</li></ol><h2 id="taxes">Which taxes and allowances</h2><p>Inheritance tax depends on the family relationship. According to the rules published by the Revenue Agency:</p><ul><li><strong>Spouse and direct relatives</strong> (children, parents): <strong>4%</strong> on the value above <strong>1 million euro per beneficiary</strong>.</li><li><strong>Brothers and sisters</strong>: <strong>6%</strong> on the value above 100,000 euro per beneficiary.</li><li>Other relatives and in-laws: 6% with no allowance. Other persons: 8%.</li></ul><p>The allowance is per heir, not on the whole estate: with a spouse and two children the total tax-free threshold is 3 million. If there are properties, mortgage tax (2%) and cadastral tax (1%) are also due, with fixed amounts in some first-home cases.</p><h2 id="late">If you go beyond 12 months</h2><p>A late or omitted return brings penalties, which for an omitted return range from 120% to 240% of the tax, plus interest. Even where no tax is due, omission can cost a minimum penalty. Better not to let the deadline pass.</p><h2 id="faq">Frequently asked questions</h2><h3>When is the succession return due?</h3><p>12 months from the opening of the succession, normally the date of death.</p><h3>If the estate is small, do I still have to file?</h3><p>It depends on the assets and the heirs: it is mandatory in many cases, especially if there are properties. Check first.</p><h3>Can I do it myself?</h3><p>Yes, but it is wise to get help if there are several heirs, properties, accounts or debts.</p><h2 id="sources">Sources</h2><ul><li><a href="https://www.redigo.info/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Agenzia-Guida-di-giugno-2026.pdf" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Agenzia delle Entrate, guide to the succession return (June 2026), in Italian</a></li><li><a href="https://www.ingenio-web.it/articoli/successione-precompilata-web-aliquote-franchigie-e-autoliquidazione-la-guida-del-fisco/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Ingenio, Pre-filled web succession: rates, allowances and self-assessment, the tax authority's guide, in Italian</a></li><li><a href="https://www.pmi.it/impresa/contabilita-e-fisco/405106/imposta-di-successione-e-donazione-aliquote-e-franchigie.html" rel="noopener" target="_blank">PMI.it, Inheritance and gift tax: rates and allowances, in Italian</a></li></ul><div class="art-nota"><p>Tax rules change often and depend on your personal situation.
