<p>Filing your tax return yourself is free. Having someone do it costs <strong>from about fifty euros to a few hundred</strong>, depending on the form and how complex it is. Here are the 2026 averages and how to work out what to spend.</p><h2>Free: the pre-filled return</h2><p>The Revenue Agency puts a ready-made return online. In 2025 <strong>5.4 million people filed Form 730 directly</strong>, 3.2 million of them with the simplified method. It works well with a single Certificazione Unica (CU) and few expenses. The risk is missing a deduction or an incorrect figure.</p><h2>What Form 730 costs</h2><ul><li>Simple 730 (one CU, no complex deductions): about <strong>40-60 euros</strong> at a CAF.</li><li>Other guides quote an average of <strong>50-70 euros</strong> for an individual 730.</li><li>Joint 730s and extra sections (land, buildings, foreign income) raise the price.</li></ul><h2>What Redditi PF costs</h2><p>According to a market guide, on average: <strong>80-140 euros</strong> for an individual without a VAT number, <strong>150-300 euros and up</strong> with a flat-rate VAT number, <strong>250-600 euros and up</strong> in complex situations. Each extra section is worth about 30-60 euros.</p><h2>If you have a VAT number</h2><p>The return is usually already inside the annual fee. For a flat-rate taxpayer a traditional firm typically charges <strong>500-1,500 euros a year</strong>, while online platforms quote 400-900 euros. For the ordinary regime it goes up to 1,000-2,400 euros.</p><p><a class="btn-cta" href="{% include url-ref.html ref="contatti" %}">Ask for a no-obligation quote</a></p><h2>How to choose</h2><ul><li><strong>One CU and simple expenses:</strong> pre-filled return or a CAF.</li><li><strong>Several CUs, rents, large deductible expenses:</strong> CAF or accountant.</li><li><strong>VAT number, foreign income, capital gains:</strong> Redditi PF with a professional.</li></ul><p>Always ask for a <strong>written quote</strong> and check whether help with Revenue Agency letters after filing is included: it is the item that varies most from firm to firm.</p><h2 id="sources">Sources</h2><ul><li><a href="https://www.ecodibergamo.it/stories/premium/Economia/dichiarazioni-dei-redditi-730-precompilato-si-parte-online-dal-30-aprile-o_3821015_11/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Eco di Bergamo, Pre-filled 730: direct filings in 2025 (Italian)</a></li><li><a href="https://lentepubblica.it/contabilita-bilancio-tasse-tributi/quanto-costa-presentare-il-modello-730-nel-2026-guida-alle-tariffe-di-caf-e-professionisti/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Lente Pubblica, What Form 730 costs in 2026 (Italian)</a></li><li><a href="https://centrofiscale.com/quanto-costa-730-caf-commercialista-2026/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Centro Fiscale, What the 730 costs in 2026 (Italian)</a></li><li><a href="https://www.fiscozen.it/guide/quanto-costa-fare-il-modello-unico-al-caf/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Fiscozen, Cost of a tax return at a CAF (Italian)</a></li><li><a href="https://www.money.it/quanto-costa-commercialista-tariffe-compensi-onorario-prezzi-2022" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Money.it, What an accountant costs (Italian)</a></li></ul><div class="art-nota"><p>Tax rules change often and depend on your personal situation.
