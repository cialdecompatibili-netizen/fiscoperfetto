<p>The <strong>administrative hold</strong> (fermo amministrativo) is a precautionary measure by which the collection agency Agenzia delle entrate-Riscossione (AdER) blocks use of a vehicle owned by someone with unpaid debts. First, though, it must send you a <strong>warning</strong>, and during that time there are several ways to avoid it.</p><h2>The stages</h2><ol><li>The tax bill arrives and 60 days pass without paying, rescheduling or challenging it.</li><li>AdER sends the <strong>hold warning</strong>: at least 30 days must pass between the warning and registration.</li><li>If nothing happens in 30 days, the hold is registered at the PRA and you can no longer drive.</li></ol><h2>How to avoid it within 30 days</h2><ul><li><strong>Pay</strong> what is due.</li><li><strong>Ask for instalments</strong> before registration.</li><li><strong>Business vehicle</strong>: if the car is used in your business or profession, you can ask for the hold not to be registered, proving it within 30 days of the warning.</li><li><strong>Challenge</strong> if the bill is wrong or was not properly served.</li></ul><h2>If the hold is already registered</h2><p>With instalments (or a tax settlement scheme), paying the first instalment suspends the hold and you can drive. Suspension can take from 48 hours to a few working days, and lapses if you miss instalments. When the debt is paid in full, AdER sends the cancellation to the PRA: you need not file a request.</p><h2>Special cases</h2><p>AdER does not register a hold on vehicles used by or intended for disabled persons and, if already registered, cancels it.</p><h2>Frequently asked questions</h2><h3>Does the hold cancel itself?</h3><p>Only when the debt is extinguished: AdER notifies the PRA.</p><h3>Can I sell the car with a hold?</h3><p>The hold is a restriction: check your situation before making any choice.</p><h3>How much debt triggers a hold?</h3><p>There is no simple rule in the sources we consulted: it depends on the debt and the rules in force.</p><h2 id="sources">Sources</h2><ul><li><a href="https://www.fiscoetasse.com/rassegna-stampa/31270-fermo-amministrativo-veicoli-tutte-le-regole-da-conoscere.html" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Fisco e Tasse, Vehicle administrative hold: all the rules (Italian)</a></li><li><a href="https://www.money.it/fermo-auto-amministrativo-2026-cos-e-come-funziona-guida" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Money.it, Car administrative hold 2026: guide (Italian)</a></li><li><a href="https://www.investireoggi.it/?p=220988" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Investireoggi, Car administrative hold 2026: suspension and requirements (Italian)</a></li></ul><div class="art-nota"><p>Tax rules change often and depend on your personal situation.
