<p>The <strong>cartella di pagamento</strong> is the document by which the collection agency Agenzia delle entrate-Riscossione (AdER) asks for sums that an authority, such as the Revenue Agency, INPS or a municipality, has entered on the tax roll. From notification you have <strong>60 days</strong> to choose what to do. If they pass with no reply, the bill becomes the basis for recovery actions. Here is how to proceed in an orderly way.</p><h2 id="check">First: check the document</h2><ul><li><strong>Which authority</strong> asked for the sums and for which tax, year and reason.</li><li><strong>Notification date</strong>: the 60 days start from there.</li><li><strong>Amounts</strong>: tax, penalties, interest, costs. Compare them with your payments (F24, receipts).</li><li><strong>Whether you already received</strong> an avviso bonario or other notices for the same debt.</li></ul><h2 id="three-ways">The three options within 60 days</h2><h3>1. Pay</h3><p>If the debt is correct and you can pay in full, pay within 60 days using the channels in the bill and keep the receipt. After the deadline, late-payment interest is added.</p><h3>2. Pay in instalments</h3><p>From the moment you file the request AdER cannot start new precautionary or enforcement procedures (vehicle holds, mortgages, garnishments), except as provided by law. According to Fisco e Tasse (27 August 2026), for requests made in 2025 and 2026:</p><ul><li>up to <strong>84 monthly instalments</strong> for debts up to 120,000 euro, with a simple statement of temporary difficulty;</li><li>up to <strong>120 instalments</strong> if you document the difficulty (ISEE for individuals, indices for businesses); documents are also needed above 120,000 euro;</li><li>minimum instalment of <strong>50 euro</strong>;</li><li>forfeiture of the plan after <strong>eight unpaid instalments</strong>, even non-consecutive: the balance becomes immediately due and you cannot reschedule those debts again.</li></ul><p>Apply from the reserved area of the AdER website (the «Rateizza adesso» service), by certified email or at the counters. Note: according to the Court of Cassation (order 27504/2024) an instalment request is an acknowledgment of the debt and interrupts the limitation period. If you think the bill is wrong or time-barred, consider this before filing.</p><h3>3. Challenge</h3><p>If you believe the sum is not due you can ask the office that issued the roll to review it (autotutela), ask for suspension of collection with documents proving the error, or file an appeal. Appeal deadlines are tight and depend on the type of tax: check the document and speak to a professional right away.</p><h2 id="mistakes">Mistakes to avoid</h2><ul><li>Letting the 60 days pass without doing anything.</li><li>Asking for instalments without checking whether the debt is correct.</li><li>Forgetting instalments: eight unpaid ones end the plan.</li><li>Paying amounts different from those stated, or through channels not provided.</li></ul><h2 id="faq">Frequently asked questions</h2><h3>What happens after 60 days?</h3><p>The bill becomes the basis for recovery actions, such as a vehicle hold, mortgage or garnishment, and late-payment interest accrues.</p><h3>Can I ask for instalments right away, without waiting 60 days?</h3><p>Yes, the request can be filed from receipt of the bill, and it blocks new actions.</p><h3>How many instalments can I get?</h3><p>It depends on the amount and your financial situation: the current limits are those above, under the rules in force on the date of the request.</p><h3>Can I ask for instalments twice?</h3><p>There is the possibility of an extension, which can be requested only once and with documents, if your situation worsens. If you forfeit the plan, you cannot reschedule the same debts again.</p><h2 id="sources">Sources</h2><ul><li><a href="https://www.fiscoetasse.com/new-rassegna-stampa/1576-rateizzazione-cartelle-quante-rate-puoi-fare-scoprilo-subito.html" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Fisco e Tasse, Tax bills 2026: up to 84 or 120 instalments (27 August 2026), in Italian</a></li><li><a href="https://www.fiscoetasse.com/approfondimenti/14241-cartelle-di-pagamento-come-fare-per-rateizzarle.html" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Fisco e Tasse, Payment bills: how to pay in instalments, in Italian</a></li><li><a href="https://www.leggioggi.it/?p=67971" rel="noopener" target="_blank">LeggiOggi, Payment bill: what it is, how to read it, what to do, in Italian</a></li></ul><div class="art-nota"><p>Tax rules change often and depend on your personal situation.
