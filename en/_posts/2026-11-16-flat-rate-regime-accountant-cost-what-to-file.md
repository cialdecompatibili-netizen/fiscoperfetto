<p>The flat-rate regime (<em>forfettario</em>) is the choice of about <strong>one new VAT number in two</strong>: in 2025 there were 242,529, or 48.5% of new openings. It looks simple, but there is still an income tax return and thresholds that, if you get them wrong, make you lose the regime.</p><h2>What an accountant costs</h2><ul><li>Traditional firm: typically <strong>500-1,500 euros a year</strong> for a flat-rate taxpayer.</li><li>Online platforms: about <strong>400-900 euros a year</strong>, often with the tax return included.</li><li>Ordinary regime: 1,000-2,400 euros a year.</li></ul><h2>What you must file</h2><p>You file the <strong>Redditi PF</strong> return with the section dedicated to the flat-rate regime. The substitute tax is <strong>15%</strong>, reduced to <strong>5% for the first five years</strong> of a new business that meets the requirements. It applies to income worked out with the <strong>profitability coefficient</strong> of your ATECO code, from 40% to 86%. The <strong>cash principle</strong> applies: revenue collected in the year counts.</p><h2>An example</h2><p>A professional (coefficient 78%) with 30,000 euros collected: income 23,400 euros; subtract 5,000 euros of INPS contributions paid and the base is 18,400 euros. The tax is <strong>920 euros at 5%</strong> or <strong>2,760 euros at 15%</strong>. This is an illustration: your numbers depend on your ATECO code and contributions.</p><p><a class="btn-cta" href="{% include url-ref.html ref="contatti" %}">Ask for a no-obligation quote</a></p><h2>The thresholds not to cross</h2><ul><li><strong>85,000 euros</strong> of revenue or fees a year to enter and stay in the regime.</li><li>If you exceed 85,000 euros during the year, you stay flat-rate up to <strong>100,000 euros</strong>.</li><li>Above 100,000 euros you leave the regime <strong>immediately</strong>.</li><li>The five years at 5% count from the opening of the VAT number.</li></ul><h2>What to ask for in the quote</h2><p>Check that the fee includes: electronic invoices, calculation and payment of tax and advance payments, Redditi PF and INPS. Also ask whether help with any Revenue Agency letters is included.</p><h2 id="sources">Sources</h2><ul><li><a href="https://www1.finanze.gov.it/finanze/osiva/public/contenuti/Sintesi_annuale_dati_2025.pdf" rel="noopener" target="_blank">MEF, VAT numbers observatory: 2025 annual summary (Italian)</a></li><li><a href="https://www.money.it/quanto-costa-commercialista-tariffe-compensi-onorario-prezzi-2022" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Money.it, What an accountant costs (Italian)</a></li><li><a href="https://centrofiscale.com/tag/costo-commercialista/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Centro Fiscale, Online accountant for flat-rate taxpayers 2026 (Italian)</a></li><li><a href="https://www.pmi.it/professioni/regole-e-compensi/350336/regime-forfettario-requisiti-limiti-cause-di-esclusione.html" rel="noopener" target="_blank">PMI.it, Flat-rate regime: requirements, limits, exclusions (Italian)</a></li><li><a href="https://fiscoinvestimenti.it/guida-regime-forfettario-2026/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Fisco Investimenti, Flat-rate regime guide 2026 (Italian)</a></li></ul><div class="art-nota"><p>Tax rules change often and depend on your personal situation.
