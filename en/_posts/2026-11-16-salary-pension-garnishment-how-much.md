<p>If the debt is with the collection agency Agenzia delle entrate-Riscossione (AdER), withholdings on salary and pension have <strong>lower limits</strong> than ordinary ones. Knowing them lets you check that what is withheld is correct.</p><h2>Salary: the three bands</h2><p>Under art. 72-ter of Presidential Decree 602/1973 (from 2026 taken up in art. 171 of the Consolidated Collection Act), the share the tax authority can garnish is:</p><ul><li><strong>one tenth</strong> if net monthly pay does not exceed 2,500 euro;</li><li><strong>one seventh</strong> between 2,500 and 5,000 euro;</li><li><strong>one fifth</strong> above 5,000 euro.</li></ul><p>For ordinary debts to private creditors the general limit is one fifth.</p><h2>Pension: the vital minimum</h2><p>The same scheme applies to pensions (one tenth, one seventh, one fifth), but a part must remain untouched: the vital minimum, at least twice the social allowance. In 2026 the social allowance is 546.24 euro per month, so double is 1,092.48 euro.</p><h2>If the money is already in the account</h2><p>When salary or pension were already credited to the account before the seizure, according to some sources a sum equal to three times the social allowance (1,638.72 euro in 2026) is protected, and the excess follows the ordinary limits. Rules on accounts and credits are complex: have them checked case by case.</p><h2>What to do</h2><ol><li>Check on your payslip how much is withheld.</li><li>Compare with the bands above.</li><li>If the withholding exceeds the limits, challenge it with documents.</li><li>Consider instalments to stop new procedures.</li></ol><h2>Frequently asked questions</h2><h3>Can they take the whole salary?</h3><p>No: there are limits by band.</p><h3>Does it apply to the 13th-month pay or severance?</h3><p>Rules vary by type of sum: ask for a check.</p><h3>What does the employer do?</h3><p>It withholds the set share and pays it to the collection agent.</p><h2 id="sources">Sources</h2><ul><li><a href="https://www.affaritaliani.it/economia/pignoramento-dello-stipendio-quanto-possono-trattenere-ogni-mese-e-quali-sono-i-limiti.html" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Affaritaliani, Salary garnishment: how much they can withhold (Italian)</a></li><li><a href="https://www.businessonline.it/news/troppi-debiti-a-che-punto-scatta-il-rischio-di-pignoramento-di-soldi-casa-e-auto-e-cosa-fare-subito-per-evitarlo_n88870.html" rel="noopener" target="_blank">BusinessOnline, Too many debts: seizure of money, home and car (Italian)</a></li><li><a href="https://quifinanza.it/fisco-tasse/leggi/svuotare-conto-corrente-reato/1004281/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">QuiFinanza, Vital minimum and bank account 2026 (Italian)</a></li></ul><div class="art-nota"><p>Tax rules change often and depend on your personal situation.
