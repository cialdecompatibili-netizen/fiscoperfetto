<p>An <strong>assessment notice</strong> (avviso di accertamento) claims additional taxes, penalties and interest. Unlike an avviso bonario, it is a real tax act, and from notification <strong>60 days</strong> run that decide a lot.</p><h2>What happens in the 60 days</h2><p>Within this period you can file an appeal. If the time passes without reacting, the act becomes final and enforceable: the amount is due and recovery can start with tax bills, holds, mortgages and other acts.</p><h2>The routes</h2><ol><li><strong>Appeal</strong> to the Tax Justice Court, within 60 days of notification.</li><li><strong>Settlement (accertamento con adesione)</strong>: you can file a request within the appeal deadline; filing suspends the deadlines for 90 days and lets you negotiate with the office.</li><li><strong>Self-review (autotutela)</strong>: ask the office to cancel or correct the act if clearly wrong.</li><li><strong>Payment</strong>, only if the debt is correct, considering the reduced penalties available to those who do not appeal.</li><li><strong>Instalments</strong> for the sums due.</li></ol><h2>Mind the deadlines</h2><p>The August suspension of deadlines (1-31 August) can add days, but do not rely on it: always count from the notification date and have the deadlines checked.</p><h2>Mistakes to avoid</h2><ul><li>Thinking it is a notice like any other and waiting.</li><li>Filing the settlement request out of time.</li><li>Paying without checking the act.</li></ul><h2>Frequently asked questions</h2><h3>Must I appeal?</h3><p>No: you can negotiate through settlement, pay or pay in instalments. It depends on the act.</p><h3>Does the appeal suspend payment?</h3><p>While proceedings are pending, provisional sums may be due: check your act.</p><h3>When do these notices arrive?</h3><p>After checks on returns, often preceded by invitations to a hearing.</p><h2 id="sources">Sources</h2><ul><li><a href="https://avvocaticartellesattoriali.com/2025/12/18/avviso-di-accertamento-esecutivo-cosa-fare-entro-60-giorni/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Enforceable assessment notice: what to do within 60 days (Italian)</a></li><li><a href="https://avvocaticartellesattoriali.com/2025/08/11/quale-procedura-deflattiva-scegliere-per-risolvere-un-avviso-di-accertamento/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Settlement procedures and the 90-day suspension (Italian)</a></li><li><a href="https://addiopignoramenti.it/2025/12/04/cosa-fare-dopo-aver-ricevuto-un-accertamento-fiscale-guida-completa/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">What to do after receiving a tax assessment (Italian)</a></li></ul><div class="art-nota"><p>Tax rules change often and depend on your personal situation.
