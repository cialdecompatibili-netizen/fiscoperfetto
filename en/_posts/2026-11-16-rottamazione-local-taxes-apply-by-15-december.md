<p>If you have old tax bills for <strong>IMU, TARI, fines or car tax</strong>, there is a window to settle them without penalties and late-payment interest. The application must be filed <strong>by 15 December 2026</strong>. It is not automatic and depends on your local authority.</p><h2>Who can join</h2><p>Only if your municipality or region decided to join (about 1,500 bodies, according to sources) and for debts entrusted to AdER between 1 January 2000 and 31 December 2023. A body may join only for some revenues: also check that your tax qualifies. Check on the Agenzia delle entrate-Riscossione site and your own local authority's site.</p><h2>What you pay</h2><ul><li><strong>IMU and TARI</strong>: principal and notification costs remain; penalties and late-payment interest are written off.</li><li><strong>Traffic fines</strong>: only interest is written off, not the fine itself.</li></ul><h2>The dates</h2><ul><li><strong>16 October - 15 December 2026</strong>: submit the application, online only on the AdER site.</li><li><strong>By 28 February 2027</strong>: AdER tells you how much you owe.</li><li><strong>By 31 March 2027</strong>: single payment, or up to 54 bimonthly instalments. Instalments carry 3% annual interest from 1 April 2027, with a 100-euro minimum.</li></ul><h2>What to do now</h2><ol><li>Check the list of participating bodies.</li><li>Check your debts in AdER's information summary.</li><li>Submit the application online before 15 December.</li><li>Keep the receipt and communications.</li></ol><h2>Frequently asked questions</h2><h3>Does it apply to every municipality?</h3><p>No, only those that joined.</p><h3>Can I supplement the application?</h3><p>Yes, by the same deadline of 15 December 2026.</p><h3>What if I already have an instalment plan?</h3><p>Rules may change: have your position checked before paying anything else.</p><h2 id="sources">Sources</h2><ul><li><a href="https://www.informazionefiscale.it/rottamazione-quinquies-tasse-locali-elenco-comuni-regioni-ader" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Informazione Fiscale, Local taxes rottamazione: list of bodies (Italian)</a></li><li><a href="https://www.money.it/mappa-della-rottamazione-quinquies-locale-comuni-aderito-domanda" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Money.it, Local rottamazione quinquies: joining municipalities (Italian)</a></li><li><a href="https://www.partitaiva.it/rottamazione-tributi-locali-elenco-comuni-aderenti/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Partitaiva.it, Local taxes rottamazione: application and deadlines (Italian)</a></li><li><a href="https://www.informazionefiscale.it/rottamazione-tasse-locali-imu-tari-multe-bollo-rata-100-euro" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Informazione Fiscale, Minimum instalment 100 euro (Italian)</a></li></ul><div class="art-nota"><p>Tax rules change often and depend on your personal situation.
