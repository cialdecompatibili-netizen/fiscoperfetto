<p>Did you send your 730 and then notice a mistake or a forgotten expense? It is not rare, and it can usually be fixed. But let us be clear: <strong>the most convenient deadlines have already passed</strong>. Today, 16 November 2026, fewer routes remain, but there is still one. Here is how to find your way.</p><h2 id="dates">The dates that mattered (already expired)</h2><ul><li><strong>26 October 2026</strong>: last day for the <strong>supplementary 730 (730 integrativo)</strong>, usable only for corrections in the taxpayer's favour (25 October fell on a Sunday).</li><li><strong>10 November 2026</strong>: deadline given for the <strong>rectifying 730 (730 rettificativo)</strong>, used when the error was made by whoever prepared it for you.</li><li><strong>2 November 2026</strong>: last day for the <strong>corrective Redditi form within the deadline</strong> (31 October fell on a Saturday).</li></ul><h2 id="whats-left">What is left today</h2><p>After 2 November 2026 only the <strong>amended Redditi Persone Fisiche form</strong> can be used. It is the Redditi form, not the 730, that you turn to when:</p><ul><li>you realise you forgot a deductible expense or tax credit;</li><li>you omitted an income or made an error that changes the tax;</li><li>you must correct after the 730 tools have expired.</li></ul><p>Deadlines depend on the type of error. For corrections <strong>against you</strong> (more tax to pay) the amended return is filed by 31 December of the fifth following year, as reported by ItaliaOggi. For corrections <strong>in your favour</strong> (more credit) the deadlines and the way to get a refund or offset must be checked case by case.</p><h2 id="what-to-do">What to do now</h2><ol><li><strong>Check what was sent</strong>: the 730 receipt and the return filed, to compare with your documents.</li><li><strong>Understand the type of error</strong>: in your favour or against you, and by how much the tax changes.</li><li><strong>Gather documents</strong>: invoices, receipts, CU, deeds.</li><li><strong>Ask for the amended Redditi form to be prepared</strong> and, if needed, payments with ravvedimento.</li></ol><h2 id="mistakes">Mistakes to avoid</h2><ul><li>Waiting and hoping nothing happens: if the error is against you, the more time passes the less chance you have of reducing penalties.</li><li>Sending a new wrong filing yourself: a second error complicates things.</li><li>Writing off a forgotten deduction: it can often be recovered, but with the right tool.</li></ul><h2 id="faq">Frequently asked questions</h2><h3>Can the supplementary 730 still be filed?</h3><p>No, the deadline was 26 October 2026.</p><h3>Can I cancel the 730?</h3><p>Cancelling was possible only within a short window after sending, which has passed.</p><h3>When is the amended Redditi form worthwhile?</h3><p>When the error is significant, in your favour or against you, and you want to regularise properly.</p><h3>How much does it cost?</h3><p>It depends on the tax assistance centre or professional and the complexity. Always ask for a quote first.</p><h2 id="sources">Sources</h2><ul><li><a href="https://www.money.it/modello-730-2026-integrativo-rettificativo-differenze" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Money.it, 730/2026 supplementary and rectifying: what are the differences, in Italian</a></li><li><a href="https://www.money.it/errori-modello-730-2026-cosa-fare" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Money.it, Errors in the 730/2026: how to fix them, in Italian</a></li><li><a href="https://www.fiscoetasse.com/new-rassegna-stampa/2773-7302026-come-correggere-errori-e-mancanze.html" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Fisco e Tasse, 730/2026: how to correct errors and omissions, in Italian</a></li><li><a href="https://www.italiaoggi.it/diritto-e-fisco/un-settembre-caldo-per-il-730-2026-xft1bkbc" rel="noopener" target="_blank">ItaliaOggi, A hot September for the 2026 730, in Italian</a></li></ul><div class="art-nota"><p>Tax rules change often and depend on your personal situation.
