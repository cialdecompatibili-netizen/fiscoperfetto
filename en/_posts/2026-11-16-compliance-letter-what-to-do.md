<p>A <strong>compliance letter</strong> (lettera di compliance) is an invitation from the Revenue Agency to check your data and, if needed, correct it yourself. <strong>It is not a tax bill, not a fine and not an assessment.</strong> In 2026 the Agency is due to send about 2.4 million: getting one does not mean you did something serious. The key is not to ignore it.</p><h2>How it arrives and what it contains</h2><p>It flags anomalies found by cross-checking data: returns, electronic invoices, receipts, payments, CU. It arrives by certified email or registered mail and can also be found in your Cassetto fiscale (tax drawer), in the «L'Agenzia scrive» section. It states the years, taxes and reason for the flag.</p><h2>What to do, in order</h2><ol><li><strong>Read it calmly</strong>: tax year, tax, anomaly indicated.</li><li><strong>Check your data</strong> against invoices, F24 payments, return and tax drawer. Sometimes the Agency is wrong: in November 2025 some VAT letters did not take all customs bills into account.</li><li><strong>If there is no anomaly</strong>: reply with documents using only the channel stated in the letter, also through your accountant.</li><li><strong>If the error is real</strong>: you can correct with ravvedimento operoso, with reduced penalties that are lower the sooner you act.</li><li><strong>Keep</strong> the letter, reply receipts and payment receipts.</li></ol><h2>If you ignore it</h2><p>An inspection with an assessment notice and higher ordinary penalties may follow. Some sources indicate 90 days to regularise with minimum penalties, but deadlines vary by letter: check the ones written in yours.</p><h2>Frequently asked questions</h2><h3>Do I have to pay right away?</h3><p>No. First check whether the flag is correct.</p><h3>Is there a deadline?</h3><p>It depends on the letter. The sooner you act, the less you pay in penalties.</p><h3>Can I reply myself?</h3><p>Yes, but if you have an intermediary it helps to involve them.</p><h2 id="sources">Sources</h2><ul><li><a href="https://www.informazionefiscale.it/controlli-fiscali-agenzia-delle-entrate-lettere-compliance-2026" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Informazione Fiscale, Tax checks: compliance letters 2026 (Italian)</a></li><li><a href="https://www.ilcommercialistaonline.it/lettera-di-compliance/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Il Commercialista Online, Compliance letter: what to do (Italian)</a></li><li><a href="https://edotto.com/articolo/compliance-iva-2025-lettere-del-fisco-per-dichiarazioni-omesse-e-altre-anomalie" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Edotto, VAT compliance: tax authority letters (Italian)</a></li><li><a href="https://www.investireoggi.it/lettere-compliance-sbagliate-arrivate-dal-fisco-cosa-fare-lo-dice-lagenzia-entrate/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Investireoggi, Wrong compliance letters: what to do (Italian)</a></li></ul><div class="art-nota"><p>Tax rules change often and depend on your personal situation.
