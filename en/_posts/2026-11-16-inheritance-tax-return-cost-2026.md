<p>It is a hard time and the last thing you need is a cost surprise. The inheritance tax return (<em>dichiarazione di successione</em>) must be filed <strong>within 12 months of death</strong>, electronically. Here is what the bill is made of.</p><h2>The cost items</h2><ul><li><strong>Fixed costs:</strong> about 245 euros across stamp duty, mortgage tax and land-registry transfer (an online calculator's estimate).</li><li><strong>CAF or accountant fee:</strong> typically 500-1,500 euros.</li><li><strong>Inheritance tax:</strong> for a spouse and children the allowance is <strong>1 million euros per heir</strong>, so the tax is often zero. For unrelated heirs the rate is 8%.</li><li><strong>Taxes on real estate:</strong> mortgage and cadastral taxes, which since 1 January 2025 must be self-assessed.</li></ul><p>A notary is not required to file the return, which can also be done by a CAF or an accountant.</p><p><a class="btn-cta" href="{% include url-ref.html ref="contatti" %}">Ask for a no-obligation quote</a></p><h2>When you are exempt</h2><p>There is an exemption only if the estate does not exceed 100,000 euros and there is no real estate, in the cases set by law. Check your own case before giving up.</p><h2>What happens after 12 months</h2><p>Penalties grow with the delay: according to the guides, within 30 days of the deadline from 60% to 120% of the tax due (minimum 250 euros), beyond that or if omitted from 120% to 240%. If there is no tax to pay the calculation changes: have a professional confirm the amount.</p><h2>Documents to prepare</h2><ul><li>death certificate and the heirs' documents;</li><li>tax codes and family relationship;</li><li>cadastral records of the properties;</li><li>bank statements, securities and details of the deceased's assets;</li><li>the will, if any.</li></ul><h2 id="sources">Sources</h2><ul><li><a href="https://sum.money/it/successione-tasse/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Sum.money, Inheritance 2026: costs, taxes and allowances (Italian)</a></li><li><a href="https://fiscoinvestimenti.it/?p=3750" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Fisco Investimenti, Inheritance tax return 2026 (Italian)</a></li><li><a href="https://dokicasa.it/documenti/successione-immobiliare-2026-documenti-scadenze" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Dokicasa, Property inheritance 2026: documents and deadlines (Italian)</a></li><li><a href="https://notaionline.it/guida/successione-costi-notaio/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">NotaiOnline, Inheritance: notary costs and taxes (Italian)</a></li><li><a href="https://www.dequo.it/articoli/dichiarazione-di-successione" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Dequo, Inheritance tax return (Italian)</a></li></ul><div class="art-nota"><p>Tax rules change often and depend on your personal situation.
