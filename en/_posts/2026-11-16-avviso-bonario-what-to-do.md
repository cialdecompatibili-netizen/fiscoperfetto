<p>The <strong>avviso bonario</strong> (courtesy notice), technically a <em>comunicazione di irregolarità</em>, is not a tax bill and not an assessment: it is the result of a check the Revenue Agency runs on your tax return. Timing is everything: if you pay within the deadline the penalty is reduced, otherwise a tax bill arrives with the full penalty. This guide tells you what to do and in which order.</p><h2 id="what">What it is and where it comes from</h2><p>It comes from automated checks (art. 36-bis of Presidential Decree 600/1973 and, for VAT, art. 54-bis of Decree 633/1972) or formal checks (art. 36-ter) on returns. Typically it flags taxes declared but not paid, insufficient payments or data that do not match. It reaches your tax domicile or your certified email; if you have an intermediary it may be sent to them electronically.</p><h2 id="deadlines">How many days you have</h2><ul><li><strong>60 days</strong> from receipt to pay or provide clarifications, for notices processed from 1 January 2025 (art. 3 of Legislative Decree 108/2024). For earlier notices the deadline was 30 days.</li><li><strong>90 days</strong> if the electronic notice was made available to the intermediary who handled the return.</li><li><strong>30 days</strong> for income taxed separately (for example severance pay and arrears), which did not get the extension.</li></ul><p>Always check the receipt date and the deadline written in your notice.</p><h2 id="what-to-do">What to do, step by step</h2><ol><li><strong>Read it calmly</strong>: amounts, tax year, tax type and the reason for the check.</li><li><strong>Compare with your records</strong>: return, F24 payments made, CU, receipts. Sometimes the payment exists but was allocated wrongly.</li><li><strong>If it is correct</strong>: pay within the deadline and get the penalty reduced to one third, or ask to pay in instalments.</li><li><strong>If you have doubts or find an error</strong>: write to the Agency with documents, including by certified email or CIVIS, before the deadline. If the Agency accepts your reasons it cancels the notice.</li></ol><h2 id="instalments">Can you pay in instalments?</h2><p>Yes. According to the Revenue Agency the amounts can be paid in <strong>up to 20 equal quarterly instalments</strong>. The first must be paid within 60 days of receipt, the following ones by the last day of each quarter. If you miss the first instalment you lose the reduced penalty, so do not put it off.</p><h2 id="mistakes">Mistakes to avoid</h2><ul><li>Ignoring the notice: the amount goes on the tax roll and a tax bill arrives with the full penalty.</li><li>Paying straight away without checking whether your payment had already been made.</li><li>Letting the days run out while looking for documents: if you need time, still reply within the deadline.</li><li>Confusing the notice with a tax bill: deadlines and defences differ.</li></ul><h2 id="faq">Frequently asked questions</h2><h3>Is an avviso bonario a tax bill?</h3><p>No. It is a preliminary notice: if you do not handle it, it can become a tax bill.</p><h3>How much do I save by paying on time?</h3><p>The penalty is reduced to one third of the ordinary one. The exact amounts are in your notice.</p><h3>Can I challenge it?</h3><p>Yes, by providing clarifications and documents to the Agency within the deadline. What is possible depends on the type of error.</p><h3>What if my accountant filed my return?</h3><p>If an intermediary filed the return, the notice may also have reached them: ask whether there is an electronic notice in your name.</p><h2 id="sources">Sources</h2><ul><li><a href="https://www.agenziaentrate.gov.it/portale/schede/pagamenti/controllo-automatico-e-formale-calcolo-rate/accedi-al-servizio-comurate" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Agenzia delle Entrate, automatic and formal checks: instalment calculation (ComuRate service), in Italian</a></li><li><a href="https://www.fiscoetasse.com/new-rassegna-stampa/1680-controllo-dichiarazione-dei-redditi-guida-ade-2025.html" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Fisco e Tasse, Tax return checks: the Revenue Agency guide 2025, in Italian</a></li><li><a href="https://www.ipsoa.it/documents/quotidiano/2024/08/10/avvisi-bonari-emessi-2025-risposte-pagamenti-60" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Ipsoa, Notices issued from 2025: replies and payments within 60 days, in Italian</a></li></ul><div class="art-nota"><p>Tax rules change often and depend on your personal situation.
