<p>Not being able to pay a tax debt happens, and <strong>ignoring it is the only move that is surely wrong</strong>. There is usually a path, but it depends on the type of document you hold. Here is the map, in order.</p><h2>1. Identify which document it is</h2><ul><li><strong>Avviso bonario</strong> (courtesy notice): you can pay in up to 20 quarterly instalments; the first is due within 60 days. See our guide on the avviso bonario.</li><li><strong>Tax bill (cartella)</strong>: 60 days to decide; up to 84 monthly instalments without documents for debts up to 120,000 euro, up to 120 with documentation. See our guide on the tax bill.</li><li><strong>Assessment</strong>: 60 days to appeal or settle.</li><li><strong>Compliance letter</strong>: often resolved sooner through ravvedimento.</li></ul><h2>2. Check the tax settlement schemes</h2><p>If you have debts entrusted to AdER from 2000 to 2023, the rottamazione-quinquies can cut the cost a lot, with payment in instalments. For local IMU, TARI and fines the application is due by 15 December 2026, if your authority joined.</p><h2>3. Choose a sustainable plan</h2><p>An instalment you cannot pay is worse than a lower, longer one. With tax-bill instalments, eight unpaid instalments end the plan; with the rottamazione-quinquies, two unpaid instalments lose the benefits.</p><h2>4. What to avoid</h2><ul><li>Doing nothing and hoping it passes.</li><li>Moving money to shield it: it can have criminal consequences too.</li><li>Paying at random without knowing which debt the sums go to.</li></ul><h2>5. If the debts are many</h2><p>If the situation is serious and the debts are numerous, there are debt-restructuring procedures: a professional can assess whether they suit your case.</p><h2>Frequently asked questions</h2><h3>Can I request more than one instalment plan?</h3><p>It depends on the document and the debt. If you forfeit a plan, you normally cannot reschedule the same debts again.</p><h3>What if I pay late?</h3><p>Interest and penalties, and the benefits of some plans can be lost: check your plan's rules.</p><h3>Where do I start?</h3><p>With the most urgent document: the one with the nearest deadline.</p><h2 id="sources">Sources</h2><ul><li><a href="https://www.fiscoetasse.com/new-rassegna-stampa/1576-rateizzazione-cartelle-quante-rate-puoi-fare-scoprilo-subito.html" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Fisco e Tasse, Tax bills 2026: up to 84 or 120 instalments (Italian)</a></li><li><a href="https://www.agenziaentrate.gov.it/portale/schede/pagamenti/controllo-automatico-e-formale-calcolo-rate/accedi-al-servizio-comurate" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Agenzia delle Entrate, automatic and formal checks: instalment calculation (Italian)</a></li><li><a href="https://www.agenziaentrateriscossione.gov.it/export/.files/it/gruppo/FAQ_Rottamazione_Quinquies.pdf" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Agenzia delle entrate-Riscossione, Rottamazione-quinquies FAQ (Italian)</a></li><li><a href="https://www.informazionefiscale.it/rottamazione-quinquies-tasse-locali-elenco-comuni-regioni-ader" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Informazione Fiscale, Local taxes rottamazione (Italian)</a></li></ul><div class="art-nota"><p>Tax rules change often and depend on your personal situation.
