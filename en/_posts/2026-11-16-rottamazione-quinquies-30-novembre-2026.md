<p>On Monday <strong>30 November 2026</strong> the <strong>third instalment of the rottamazione-quinquies</strong> falls due: the tax-debt settlement scheme for unpaid tax bills introduced by the 2026 Budget Law (law no. 199/2025). For this instalment there is <strong>no five-day grace period</strong>, and missing several instalments means losing the benefits. This guide covers what is paid, the dates, how to pay and what happens if you get it wrong, following the rules published by the Italian collection agency Agenzia delle entrate-Riscossione (AdER).</p><h2 id="what">What the rottamazione-quinquies is and which debts it covers</h2><p>It covers amounts entrusted to the collection agent between <strong>1 January 2000 and 31 December 2023</strong> arising from unpaid:</p><ul><li>taxes that emerged from the Revenue Agency's automated and formal checks of annual returns;</li><li>social security contributions owed to INPS, excluding those requested following an assessment;</li><li>administrative fines for Highway Code violations.</li></ul><p>You settle the debt by paying only the <strong>principal</strong> and the sums for enforcement costs and notification fees. Interest entered on the tax roll, penalties, late-payment interest and collection fees are not due. For traffic fines the scheme applies only to interest and collection fees.</p><h2 id="deadlines">The payment plan deadlines</h2><p>Those who chose a single payment paid by 31 July 2026 (5 August with the grace period). Those who chose instalments, up to 54 bimonthly payments with a minimum of 100 euro each, must follow this calendar:</p><ul><li><strong>1st instalment:</strong> 31 July 2026</li><li><strong>2nd instalment:</strong> 30 September 2026</li><li><strong>3rd instalment: 30 November 2026</strong></li><li><strong>4th to 51st:</strong> 31 January, 31 March, 31 May, 31 July, 30 September and 30 November of each year from 2027</li><li><strong>52nd to 54th:</strong> 31 January, 31 March and 31 May 2035</li></ul><p>Instalments carry <strong>interest of 3% a year from 1 August 2026</strong>. Your exact dates and amounts are the ones in the <em>Comunicazione delle somme dovute</em> (statement of sums due) that AdER sent by 30 June 2026 together with the payment slips. 30 November 2026 is a Monday: the date does not move.</p><h2 id="three-deadlines">Three different instalments fall due on 30 November</h2><p>On the same day you must also pay instalments of the older rottamazione-quater, according to Informazione Fiscale:</p><ul><li>the <strong>third instalment</strong> of the rottamazione-quinquies;</li><li>the <strong>fourteenth instalment</strong> of the rottamazione-quater;</li><li>the <strong>sixth instalment</strong> of the re-admission to the rottamazione-quater.</li></ul><p>The rules on late payment differ. Quater and re-admission have the five-day grace period: the 30 November instalment can be paid by 5 December, which is a Saturday and therefore moves to <strong>Monday 7 December 2026</strong>. For the quinquies the payment must be made by 30 November, with no margin.</p><h2 id="grace">Five-day grace period: who it really applies to</h2><p>For the quinquies the law grants five days of grace <strong>only for the single payment</strong> (chosen by those who paid in one go) or <strong>only for the last instalment</strong> of the plan. For all intermediate instalments, including the one on 30 November 2026, the deadline is strict.</p><h2 id="forfeiture">When you lose the scheme</h2><p>The settlement becomes ineffective if there is a missed or insufficient payment of:</p><ul><li>the single payment chosen (or a payment more than five days late);</li><li><strong>two instalments</strong>, even non-consecutive, if you chose to pay in instalments;</li><li>the last instalment of the plan (or a payment more than five days late).</li></ul><p>So with the quinquies you can <strong>fall behind by one instalment only</strong> without forfeiting. Mind the mechanism: when you pay the instalment after the one you skipped, the amount paid first covers the instalment left behind. AdER gives this example with a three-instalment plan: if you pay the first and the third (which is also the last) and skip the second, the third payment is applied to the second and the last one is in effect unpaid. The result is forfeiture.</p><h2 id="effects">What happens if you forfeit</h2><ul><li>Payments already made stay, but count as an <strong>advance payment</strong> on the total sums due.</li><li>Limitation and forfeiture periods start running again and the collection agent can start new precautionary or enforcement procedures.</li><li>The debts <strong>can no longer be paid in ordinary instalments</strong> (art. 19 of Presidential Decree 602/1973).</li></ul><p>For the rottamazione-quater, by contrast, you lose the benefits after a single missed payment or one more than five days late, but you can ask for a new ordinary instalment plan on the remainder.</p><table><thead><tr><th>Aspect</th><th>Rottamazione-quater</th><th>Rottamazione-quinquies</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>5-day grace period</td><td>On all instalments</td><td>Only for the single payment or the last instalment</td></tr><tr><td>When you forfeit</td><td>A single missed payment or one more than 5 days late</td><td>Two unpaid instalments (even non-consecutive) or the last instalment</td></tr><tr><td>After forfeiture</td><td>Ordinary instalments possible on the remainder</td><td>No ordinary instalments</td></tr></tbody></table><h2 id="how-to-pay">How to pay</h2><p>Use the payment slips attached to the statement of sums due. The channels listed by AdER are:</p><ul><li>the Agenzia delle entrate-Riscossione website and the EquiClick app;</li><li>direct debit from your bank account, following the statement's instructions;</li><li>bank counters, post offices, online banking, tobacconists and lottery shops;</li><li>ATMs that support CBILL and Postamat services;</li><li>AdER counters, by booking an appointment.</li></ul><p>If you no longer have the statement you can retrieve it from the reserved area of the AdER website. The updated calendar of all settlement schemes is on the <em>Prossime scadenze</em> (upcoming deadlines) page of the AdER site.</p><h2 id="checklist">What to do before 30 November</h2><ul><li>Log in to the AdER reserved area and check the amount and due date of each instalment.</li><li>Check which instalments you have already paid: if you skipped one, the November payment first covers that one.</li><li>Make sure your account has enough funds, especially if you set up direct debit.</li><li>If you also have the quater or the re-admission, keep the payment slips separate: the late-payment rules are not the same.</li><li>Pay a few days early: the quinquies has no grace period.</li></ul><p>The second income-tax instalment and the stamp duty on electronic invoices are also due on 30 November: see the guide to the <a href="{{ '/en/blog/tax-returns/second-acconto-30-november-2026/' | relative_url }}">second acconto</a>.</p><h2 id="faq">Frequently asked questions</h2><h3>When is the third instalment of the rottamazione-quinquies due?</h3><p>Monday 30 November 2026, with no five-day grace period.</p><h3>If I skip an instalment do I forfeit immediately?</h3><p>No: in the quinquies forfeiture happens with two unpaid instalments, even non-consecutive, or with a missed final instalment. But the next payment first covers the instalment left behind.</p><h3>Can I pay the 30 November instalment on 5 December?</h3><p>Only for the rottamazione-quater and the re-admission, where grace runs until Monday 7 December 2026. Not for the quinquies.</p><h3>If I forfeit the quinquies can I pay the debt in instalments?</h3><p>No, the debts can no longer be paid with ordinary instalments. Payments already made stay as an advance.</p><h3>How much interest is charged?</h3><p>Instalments carry 3% interest a year from 1 August 2026. Interest entered on the roll, penalties and collection fees are not due.</p><h2 id="sources">Sources</h2><ul><li><a href="https://www.agenziaentrateriscossione.gov.it/export/.files/it/gruppo/FAQ_Rottamazione_Quinquies.pdf" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Agenzia delle entrate-Riscossione, FAQ on the settlement scheme (Rottamazione-quinquies), in Italian</a></li><li><a href="https://www.agenziaentrateriscossione.gov.it/it/prossime-scadenze/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Agenzia delle entrate-Riscossione, calendar of upcoming deadlines, in Italian</a></li><li><a href="https://www.informazionefiscale.it/rottamazione-cartelle-scadenza-30-novembre-2026-quinquies-quater" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Informazione Fiscale, Rottamazione cartelle: tris di scadenze a novembre 2026 (26 October 2026), in Italian</a></li></ul><div class="art-nota"><p>Tax rules change often and depend on your personal situation.
