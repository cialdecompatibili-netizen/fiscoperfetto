<p>From 2026 the Revenue Agency can <strong>automatically reconstruct and assess VAT</strong> for anyone who did not file the annual return, using data it already holds: electronic invoices issued and received, receipts, periodic VAT settlement communications (LIPE) and payments already made. The first roughly 1,400 letters have gone out, and they are not mere invitations: according to the press they are acts with enhanced enforceability.</p><h2>What to do within 60 days</h2><p>From receipt you have <strong>60 days to pay or flag any errors</strong>. If you do nothing, the procedure can continue to collection. According to a reconstruction of the 28 August 2026 order, paying within 60 days reduces penalties (to 40%).</p><h2>Practical steps</h2><ol><li><strong>Read the letter</strong> and identify the year and amount.</li><li><strong>Check whether the return was really omitted</strong>: was it filed? Keep the receipt.</li><li><strong>Compare the data</strong> the Agency used with your books: invoices, LIPE, F24.</li><li><strong>If the data are wrong</strong>, flag it with documents within the 60 days.</li><li><strong>If VAT is due</strong>, consider paying or instalments.</li><li><strong>File the return</strong> with your professional if it is missing.</li></ol><h2>Frequently asked questions</h2><h3>Is it a compliance letter?</h3><p>No: it has stronger effects.</p><h3>What counts as Agency data?</h3><p>Electronic invoices, receipts, LIPE and payments.</p><h3>Can I pay in instalments?</h3><p>Yes, under the instalment rules in force: ask for a check.</p><h2 id="sources">Sources</h2><ul><li><a href="https://tg24.sky.it/economia/2026/10/12/evasione-fiscale-recupero-iva" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Sky TG24, Tax evasion: letters to recover VAT (Italian)</a></li><li><a href="https://www.informazionefiscale.it/dichiarazione-iva-omessa-liquidazione-automatica-agenzia-entrate-novita" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Informazione Fiscale, Omitted VAT return: automatic assessment (Italian)</a></li><li><a href="https://www.mysolution.it/fisco/approfondimenti/circolare-monografica/2026/09/dichiarazione-iva-omessa-nuova-liquidazione-automatizzata-e-recupero-dellimposta-dai-dati-gia-disponibili-allagenzia-setti/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">MySolution, Omitted VAT: new automated assessment (order of 28 August 2026), Italian</a></li></ul><div class="art-nota"><p>Tax rules change often and depend on your personal situation.
