<p>Every year about half a million VAT numbers are opened in Italy, and the sector you start in changes how much you pay quite a lot. Here are the figures from the Ministry of Economy and Finance (MEF) observatory and a concrete comparison.</p><h2>Where new VAT numbers are opened</h2><ul><li><strong>2025:</strong> 500,341 new openings (+0.4%), 242,529 of them flat-rate (48.5%). First sector professional activities (16.6%), then retail (16%) and construction (9.6%).</li><li><strong>Second quarter 2026:</strong> 124,170 openings (+2.2%), flat-rate at 48.7%. Professions 16.8%, retail 15.3%, construction 9.4%.</li><li><strong>First quarter 2026:</strong> healthcare and social care also stand out, at 12.5% of openings.</li></ul><p>The signal: professions have overtaken retail as the leading starting sector, and the flat-rate regime is the choice of almost one new VAT number in two.</p><h2>Same revenue, different tax</h2><p>In the flat-rate regime tax is calculated on income obtained with the <strong>profitability coefficient</strong>: 78% for professional activities, 40% for retail, 86% for construction and real-estate activities. With <strong>50,000 euros</strong> collected and a 15% rate (before INPS contributions):</p><ul><li>professional: income 39,000 euros, tax <strong>5,850 euros</strong>;</li><li>retail: income 20,000 euros, tax <strong>3,000 euros</strong>;</li><li>construction or real estate: income 43,000 euros, tax <strong>6,450 euros</strong>.</li></ul><p><a class="btn-cta" href="{% include url-ref.html ref="contatti" %}">Ask for a no-obligation quote</a></p><h2>What to do in the first year</h2><ul><li>Check your <strong>ATECO code</strong>: it decides the coefficient.</li><li>Consider the <strong>5%</strong> rate for the first five years, if you qualify.</li><li>Keep collections and invoices distinct: the cash principle applies.</li><li>Plan the Redditi PF return and advance payments, so you do not face one big bill all at once.</li></ul><h2 id="sources">Sources</h2><ul><li><a href="https://www1.finanze.gov.it/finanze/osiva/public/contenuti/Sintesi_annuale_dati_2025.pdf" rel="noopener" target="_blank">MEF, VAT numbers observatory: 2025 annual summary (Italian)</a></li><li><a href="https://www1.finanze.gov.it/finanze/osiva/public/contenuti/Sintesi_mensile_dei_dati.pdf" rel="noopener" target="_blank">MEF, VAT numbers observatory: second quarter 2026 (Italian)</a></li><li><a href="https://www.pmi.it/?p=493995" rel="noopener" target="_blank">PMI.it, VAT numbers observatory: first quarter 2026 (Italian)</a></li><li><a href="https://www.partitaiva.it/dati-partite-iva-secondo-trimestre-2026/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Partitaiva.it, VAT number data second quarter 2026 (Italian)</a></li><li><a href="https://www.pmi.it/professioni/regole-e-compensi/350336/regime-forfettario-requisiti-limiti-cause-di-esclusione.html" rel="noopener" target="_blank">PMI.it, Flat-rate regime: profitability coefficients (Italian)</a></li></ul><div class="art-nota"><p>Tax rules change often and depend on your personal situation.
